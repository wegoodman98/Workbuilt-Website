BEFORE YOU REACH OUT

Common questions about the pilot.

The first conversation is about understanding the problem, not selling you a package.

What does the first pilot cost?

Nothing. If we identify a problem that's a good fit for the Workbuilt pilot, the first small improvement is evaluated and tested at no charge.

Do I have to buy software?

No. Workbuilt starts with the problem. The right answer may be a process change, something you already use, automation, AI, new software, or a combination.

What kind of problem should I bring?

Anything that repeatedly wastes staff time, causes missed follow-up, creates delays or mistakes, frustrates customers, or depends too heavily on someone remembering to do the next step.

What happens after I submit the form?

We'll have a short conversation to understand the problem. If there's a promising opportunity, we'll document how it works today and decide together whether a small pilot makes sense.

What if the problem isn't a good fit?

We'll tell you. The goal is not to force every problem into a Workbuilt project. If we don't see a practical way to help, there's no obligation.
