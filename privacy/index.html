Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026

Overview

Workbuilt helps businesses identify process problems, test practical improvements, and measure results. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected and used when you visit getworkbuilt.com, submit information through the website, communicate with Workbuilt, or authorize a connected service.

Information we collect

We may collect information you choose to provide, including your name, business name, email address, phone number, business problem descriptions, process information, and other details submitted through forms, email, phone, or other direct communications.

We may also receive limited technical information such as browser type, device information, referral source, page activity, and similar website analytics data when those services are enabled.

Google account and Google Ads API data

If Workbuilt connects to Google services using OAuth, access is limited to the permissions you authorize. Workbuilt may use authorized Google Ads API data to support market research, advertising analysis, campaign-related workflows, reporting, and related Workbuilt services.

Workbuilt does not sell Google user data. Google account access is not used for advertising unrelated third-party products, credit decisions, or other unrelated purposes.

How information is used

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, evaluate business problems, operate pilots or implementations, provide requested services, improve Workbuilt's systems and website, maintain security, measure results, and communicate about an active or requested Workbuilt engagement.

Sharing of information

Workbuilt does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the website or deliver requested services, or when disclosure is required by law.

Data retention

Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to support an active business relationship, maintain business records, resolve disputes, or meet legal and security obligations.

Security

Workbuilt uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards intended to protect information. No website, system, or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices

You may contact Workbuilt to request correction or deletion of information you previously provided, subject to legal, security, and legitimate business-record requirements. You may also revoke Google account access through your Google account permissions at any time.

Third-party services

The Workbuilt website or services may use third-party platforms such as Google, Formspree, GitHub, or other infrastructure and analytics providers. Their own privacy terms apply to information processed by those services.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Workbuilt's services and integrations change. The current version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date when appropriate.
